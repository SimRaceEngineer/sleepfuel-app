Sleep Fuel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Sleep Fuel is a caffeine tracking application developed by Jerome Brivet. This privacy policy describes how Sleep Fuel handles your data.

What we collect (all stored locally on your device)

  • The caffeinated drinks you log (name, amount in mg, time)
  • Your personal profile (caffeine half-life, sleep threshold, daily goal, bedtime, wake-up time)
  • Your unlocked achievement badges and streaks
  • App preferences (language, theme)

None of this data leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (e.g. tapping the Share button on a badge image).

Health Connect (optional)

With your explicit permission, Sleep Fuel can:

  • Write your caffeine intake to Android Health Connect as Nutrition records.
  • Read your recent sleep data from Health Connect to correlate caffeine consumption with sleep quality.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect → Sleep Fuel.

Wear OS synchronization

If you pair a Wear OS smartwatch with your Android phone, Sleep Fuel syncs your caffeine logs and app state between phone and watch using Google's Wearable Data Layer API. This data flows directly between your two devices via Google's secure system — no third-party server is involved.

Barcode scanner

When you scan a product barcode, the app sends only the numeric barcode to the public OpenFoodFacts API to look up the product name and caffeine content. No personal data is transmitted.

No account, no servers, no analytics

Sleep Fuel does not require an account, does not operate any backend server, and does not collect analytics, crash reports, or telemetry. We do not track you. We do not show ads.

Children

Sleep Fuel is intended for users 13 years and older. The app discusses caffeine, which is not recommended for younger children.

Data deletion

All your data is stored locally on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app from Android Settings → Apps → Sleep Fuel → Uninstall. Health Connect data must be deleted separately via the Health Connect app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
